Schedule

MySQL is dumped with mysqldump --single-transaction, PostgreSQL with pg_dump, and SQLite copied with .backup, so apps keep running while backups are taken. Files are gzipped into /var/backups/skiff, readable by root only.

Backups run one at a time, alongside deploys.
